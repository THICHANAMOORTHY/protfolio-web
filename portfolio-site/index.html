01. About Me

I'm a final-year B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering student at EASA College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous), Coimbatore, affiliated with Anna University.

Embedded systems are what I enjoy most. I build them with ESP32 and Embedded C: wiring up sensors over UART, I2C and SPI, driving displays, motors and I2S audio, and getting real-time data to a dashboard. At my Emertxe internship I built an EV dashboard with ADAS warnings.

I also design digital hardware in Verilog and bring it up on Artix-7 FPGAs, from RTL and testbenches to Python-based verification against reference models. I'm currently looking for embedded systems and FPGA fresher roles.
